CAVEAT: on this SIMULATED data the break at day 30 is partly built in -- 00_make_dataset.py draws 'repeat within 30 days' gaps from a 1-30 day distribution. So this chart illustrates the method; it does not independently validate the 30-day window. On real data, pick the window from this distribution (and see the 60/90-day sensitivity in section 4.1).
